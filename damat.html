Order of Events

A celebration spanning the Bosphorus sunset to midnight

4:00 PM – 4:30 PM
Guest Arrival & Welcome Refreshments
Guests are welcomed at the Imperial Gate with vintage champagne, iced fruit infusions, and gentle classical string quartet melodies.
4:30 PM – 5:30 PM
The Nuptial Ceremony
Exchange of marriage vows and rings on the open-air Bosphorus Marble Terrace overlooking the Asian coastline.
5:30 PM – 7:00 PM
Golden Hour Cocktails & Canapés
Sunset cocktails, artisanal Mediterranean canapés, and live jazz trio on the palace fountain gardens.
7:00 PM – 9:30 PM
Five-Course Royal Gala Banquet
Seated gourmet dinner inside the crystal-chandeliered Sultan Ballroom, accompanied by family speeches and celebratory toasts.
9:30 PM
Cutting of the Cake & First Dance
The newlyweds take the floor for their first dance, followed by celebratory champagne and wedding cake service.
10:00 PM – Late
The Grand Celebration & After-Party
Live international orchestra, dancing, late-night gourmet Turkish street food bar, and celebration into the evening.
