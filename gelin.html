The Henna Tradition Explained

A heritage dating back centuries in Mediterranean and Ottoman culture

Symbolism of the Henna Ceremony

In Turkish wedding tradition, the Henna Night (Kına Gecesi) is an intimate celebration symbolizing devotion, fertility, and protection for the bride before she enters her new home. As a sign of prosperity, the mother-in-law places a gold coin into the bride's palm before the organic botanical henna is wrapped in red silk.

The Halay Circle Dance

All guests, international and local alike, join hand-in-hand to participate in the joyous Halay circle dance, led by the rhythmic pulse of acoustic drums and woodwinds along the waterfront terrace.
